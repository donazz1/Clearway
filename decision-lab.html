How this is calculated
Gross fares = hours/week × fares/hour × avg fare. Driver share = gross fares × your revenue split %. Operating cost = hours/week × avg speed (km/h) × cost per km. Net = driver share − operating cost. This is a from-scratch estimate — there's no real driver-operated Tesla network to benchmark against, so treat every input as a guess you can adjust.
Grounded in Cern Basher's (@CernBasher on X) published Cybercab/Robotaxi modeling: a $3 base + $1/mile fare, a baseline 35% vehicle utilization (8.4 hrs/day in service), and Tesla take rates ranging 15–50%. He calls a 35% take rate — Tesla 35%, owner 65% — "a pretty good mix."
