Rideshare / robotaxi income

If part of the reason to switch is future network income, model it here — separately from the core decision above.
"Active" means a human drives; "Passive" means the car drives itself — everything else (fare, split, cost) is one shared model either way, so you can shape it to whatever arrangement actually applies. Most people are some mix of private clientele (100% theirs) and platform trips (a cut goes to whoever runs the network — 70% yours / 30% platform by default, a placeholder in the range analysts like Cern Basher (@CernBasher on X) have floated for Tesla specifically) — set the mix below to match reality. Passive mode's real unlock either way is hours: a car doesn't get tired, so it can serve more trips than one person driving ever could.

How this is calculated
Gross fares = hours/week × fares/hour × avg fare. Your blended share = (% private × 100%) + (% platform × your network share) — shown below the fields. Your take = gross fares × blended share. Operating cost = hours/week × avg speed (km/h) × cost per km. Net = your take − operating cost. In passive mode, "hours/week" is the vehicle's time earning instead of your own — same formula either way, since taking yourself out of the driver's seat doesn't change the fare economics, only whose hours they are and how many of them are realistic.
