How this is calculated
Paid hours = clock hours/week × paid-hour factor (time actually earning — the rest is idle time between trips, charging, cleaning, weather). Gross fares = paid hours × fares/hour × avg fare. Your share = (% private × 100%) + (% platform × your network split) — locked to the network split alone in Tesla Network mode, since a dispatched network car isn't running private side jobs. Running cost = paid hours × avg speed (km/h) × cost per km — state below whether that includes depreciation. Net/week = (fares × share) − running cost − (car payment ÷ 4.333). Net/month = that × 4.333. Break-even is the clock-hours/week where net crosses $0, marked on the slider.
